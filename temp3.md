the 0.5-noise run did (configs 12-09-2026-07-10 and 07-11, run 12-09-18-02_…stage2 then 12-09-18-14_…stage3):

1. Data. HARD-V3: 50 training flights of 10 s at $h=0.01$ s (CF2P in PyBullet at 1000 Hz, gusts off, recovery and yaw kicks recorded in the wrench), 10 clean test flights. The training flights carry additive Gaussian noise of σ = 0.5 on position, body velocity and body rate, and rotation noise $R\exp(\hat\eta)$ with $\eta\sim\mathcal N(0,0.5^2I)$, applied once to the full flights by the generator (train-obs-noise-absolute0p5.pkl); no loader noise. For comparison, the velocities in the data have a median of 0.5 m/s, so the noise is as large as the typical signal.
2. Windows. Cut from the noisy flights with stride 10 (overlapping): stage 1 uses 51-point windows (0.5 s, 4800 windows), stage 2 uses 101-point windows (1 s, 4550 windows). Minibatches of 256 windows drawn without replacement each step.
3. Model. Learned levels plus GP residuals for $M_1^{-1}$, $M_2^{-1}$, $D_v$, $D_\omega$, $g$; the potential is the structured $V=g,z/\mu(x)$ with $g=9.81$ and the learned $\mu(x)=e^{\lambda_0+f_{M_1}(x)}$ (gp.structured_potential: true), so the $V$ GP and its level are unused and its level is frozen in the optimiser. No gravity penalty, no level prior.
4. Loss. SE(3) Gaussian NLL with learned per-block σ, initialised at 0.5 (gp.initial_observation_sigma: 0.5), plus the GP KL spread over all transitions ($\beta=1$, annealed over the first 1000 steps), plus the latent-initial-state term: every window $i$ has $q(x_0^{(i)})=\mathcal N(x_0^{\text{obs}}+m_i,s_i^2)$ over the 12 tangent coordinates, sampled by reparameterisation before the rollout, with $\mathrm{KL}(q|\mathcal N(x_0^{\text{obs}},0.5^2))$ added per window (latent_initial_state_sigma: 0.5, i.e. the prior width equals the noise level). Full backpropagation through the window's Lie-IMEX rollout.
5. Optimiser. Prodigy (η = 0.5, $d_0=10^{-3}$, safeguard warm-up) on the level scalars, Adam $10^{-3}$ for GP weight means, log-stds and σ, Adam $10^{-2}$ for the latent means and log-stds, global gradient-norm clip at 10.
6. Schedule (curriculum B). Stage 1: 3000 steps at K=50 from random initialisation; stage 2: 5000 steps at K=100 starting from the stage-1 parameters with a fresh optimiser and freshly initialised latents (the window set changes). Evaluation every 250 steps: window losses, the 1 s and 3 s open-loop position RMS on the clean test flights, the latent statistics.
7. What the log showed. No non-finite step at any point (the earlier 0.5-noise runs without the latent state diverged twice). The latent velocity corrections settled at 0.48 m/s RMS, i.e. they absorbed the 0.5 noise, with posterior width narrowing to 0.16; σ converged to 0.50–0.52; the 1 s / 3 s test errors ended at 0.25 / 0.58 m.
8. Report. Same 47-page report as the other runs on the 3 s clean 240 Hz reference (posterior-mean rollout, ±2σ band over 10 posterior weight samples), plus the 20 s diamond with the energy-based controller with the learned damping feedforward (report.controller_use_dissipation: true), which is the row that fails at this noise level; the reference-law number (0.064 m) came from the separate diagnostic run.

Nothing in the training pipeline is noise-specific except the two σ-scaled settings in step 4 (prior width and initial σ); everything else is identical to the 0.1-noise run.

User: how was this obs noise 0.5 training done?

Claude: The 0.5-noise run used the HARD-V3 flights with σ = 0.5 additive noise on position, velocities and rotation tangent, overlapping 0.5 s then 1 s windows (curriculum B, 3000 + 5000 steps, fresh optimiser per stage), the structured potential $V=g,z/\mu(x)$, the SE(3) NLL with learned σ started at 0.5, the GP KL, and a per-window latent initial state with prior width 0.5 equal to the noise, trained with Prodigy on levels and Adam elsewhere; only the two σ-scaled settings differ from the 0.1-noise run, and it trained without any divergence.
